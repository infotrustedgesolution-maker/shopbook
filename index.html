Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Who runs ShopBook

ShopBook is developed and operated by TrustEdge Solution. For any privacy questions, contact us at contact.trustedgesolution@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Google account info (name, email) when you sign in.
  • Shop data you enter: products, stock, bills, customer names/phone numbers, ledger (khata) entries, and shop profile details (name, address, phone, UPI ID, GST number).
  • Contacts — only if you choose to pick a customer from your phone's contacts. We only read the one contact you select (name and phone number) to fill in the customer form; we never read your contacts in bulk or send them anywhere beyond your own shop's customer record.
  • Basic device/usage data collected automatically by Firebase and the ad network described below.

How we use it

Your shop data is used solely to provide the app's features — inventory, billing, khata book, staff access, and dashboard reports — for your shop. It is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and is only accessible to your shop's owner and the employees you explicitly invite.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and app security (App Check).
  • Google AdMob — shows ads in the app; may collect advertising identifiers per Google's own privacy policy.

We do not sell your data to third parties.

Notifications

ShopBook schedules local reminder notifications on your device for upcoming/overdue khata book payments. These are generated and shown entirely on-device.

SMS

When you add a khata (credit/debit) entry, ShopBook can open your phone's own SMS app with a message pre-filled for that customer. ShopBook never sends SMS on its own — you always choose whether to tap Send in your own messaging app.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account/shop exists. To request deletion of your shop's data, contact us at contact.trustedgesolution@gmail.com.
