Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who runs ShopBook

ShopBook is developed and operated by TrustEdge Solution. For any privacy questions, contact us at contact.trustedgesolution@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Google account info (name, email) when you sign in.
  • Shop data you enter: items and stock, bills (with the customer's name, and the vehicle number for garages), customer names and phone numbers, ledger entries and their due dates, and shop details (name, country, currency, address, phone, UPI ID, tax number, business type and industry).
  • Job cards and repairs, if your shop uses them: customer name and phone, vehicle number, make, model and odometer, or device model and IMEI, the complaint or problem, parts and labour, and who the job is assigned to.
  • Serial numbers and IMEIs of units you stock and sell, with the bill they were sold on and their warranty end date.
  • Staff profile details — if you're a staff member, the name, age, blood group, phone numbers and addresses you choose to enter about yourself. This is optional, visible only to you and your shop's owner, and only you can edit or delete it.
  • Who created each bill and job — the app records which signed-in account made it, so the owner can see what each staff member did.
  • Barcodes you scan — read through your camera to find or add items. We don't store photos or video, only the decoded number.
  • Nearby Bluetooth devices — only if you connect a receipt printer, and only to find and print to it.
  • Contacts — only when you pick a customer from your phone's contacts. We read only the contact you choose (name and phone).
  • Basic device and usage data collected automatically by Firebase and the ad network described below.

How we use it

Your shop data is used only to provide the app's features — billing, stock, ledger, job cards and repairs, staff access and reports — for your shop. It is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and is only accessible to your shop's owner and the staff they invite. The owner decides what each staff member can see and do.

If your shop links a shared ledger with another ShopBook shop, only your shop name and phone number are made discoverable for that purpose — never your other shop data.

Permissions this app requests

  • Camera — to scan barcodes and IMEIs.
  • Bluetooth — to find and print on a paired receipt printer.
  • Contacts — only when you choose to pick a customer from your contacts.
  • Notifications — for on-device reminders about upcoming or overdue payments.

Each is used only for the feature above, never in the background without your action.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — sign-in, database and app security (App Check).
  • Google AdMob — shows ads in the app and may collect advertising identifiers under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Open Food Facts — when you scan a barcode, only that number is sent to this public product database to try to fill in the item's name. No personal or shop data is sent.

We do not sell your data.

Notifications

ShopBook schedules payment reminders on your device. They are created and shown entirely on the device.

SMS

ShopBook can open your phone's own SMS app with a message filled in for a customer. It never sends SMS by itself — you always choose whether to send.

Data on your device

So the app keeps working without internet, a copy of your shop data is kept on your device and synced with Firebase when you're back online. Printer and receipt settings are stored only on the device. Deleting your account clears this copy the next time the app starts.

Exporting and restoring your data

A shop owner can export the shop's data at any time from Settings → Backup & export — for this week, this month, last month or all time — as a backup file and spreadsheet (CSV) files. The files are made on your device and go only where you choose to send them. Restoring a backup adds its data back to your shop; nothing already there is changed or removed.

Data retention & account deletion

Your data is kept as long as your account and shop exist.

Delete your account in the app: open ShopBook and go to Settings → Delete account. You'll be asked to confirm and to choose your Google account again.

  • Shop owner: your login and every shop you own are permanently deleted — bills, items and stock, customers, ledger, job cards, serial numbers and staff profiles — and all staff lose access.
  • Staff member: your login and staff profile are deleted and you're removed from the shops you work in. Bills you made stay with the shop as part of its records.

Deleted data can't be recovered.

While your shop exists, bills are never deleted, only marked cancelled, so your accounts stay complete. Each bill keeps the name of the customer it was made for, even if you later remove that customer from your list.

Can't open the app? Email contact.trustedgesolution@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, asking for your account to be deleted. We'll delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.
