Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Who runs ShopBook

ShopBook is developed and operated by TrustEdge Solution. For any privacy questions, contact us at contact.trustedgesolution@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Google account info (name, email) when you sign in.
  • Shop data you enter: products, stock, bills, customer names/phone numbers, ledger entries, and shop profile details (name, address, phone, UPI ID, GST number, business type/industry).
  • Staff profile details — if you're an employee, the name, age, blood group, mobile/alternate mobile number, and current/permanent address you choose to enter about yourself. This is optional, visible only to you and your shop's owner, and only you can edit or delete it.
  • Who created each bill — when a bill is saved, the app records which signed-in account (owner or staff) created it, so the shop owner can see what each employee has sold.
  • Barcodes you scan — read via your camera to look up or search products; we don't store photos or video, only the decoded barcode number.
  • Nearby Bluetooth devices — only if you connect a POS receipt printer; we use this solely to find and print to that printer, never for location tracking.
  • Contacts — only if you choose to pick a customer from your phone's contacts. We only read the one contact you select (name and phone number) to fill in the customer form; we never read your contacts in bulk or send them anywhere beyond your own shop's customer record.
  • Basic device/usage data collected automatically by Firebase and the ad network described below.

How we use it

Your shop data is used solely to provide the app's features — inventory, billing, ledger, staff access, and dashboard reports — for your shop. It is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and is only accessible to your shop's owner and the employees you explicitly invite. An owner can also grant or restrict what each employee can access (billing, inventory, cost/profit figures) and see the bills that employee has created.

If your shop shares its phone number with another ShopBook shop to link a shared ledger, only your shop name and phone number are made discoverable for that purpose — never your full shop data.

Permissions this app requests

  • Camera — to scan product/item barcodes.
  • Bluetooth — to find and print receipts on a paired POS thermal printer.
  • Contacts — only when you tap "Pick from Contacts" while adding a customer.
  • Notifications — to show on-device reminders for upcoming/overdue ledger payments.

Each of these is used only for the specific feature above, never in the background without your action.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and app security (App Check).
  • Google AdMob — shows ads in the app; may collect advertising identifiers per Google's own privacy policy.
  • Open Food Facts — when you scan a barcode, we send only that barcode number to this public product database to try to auto-fill the item's name/category. No personal or shop data is sent.

We do not sell your data to third parties.

Notifications

ShopBook schedules local reminder notifications on your device for upcoming/overdue ledger payments. These are generated and shown entirely on-device.

SMS

When you add a ledger (credit/debit) entry, ShopBook can open your phone's own SMS app with a message pre-filled for that customer. ShopBook never sends SMS on its own — you always choose whether to tap Send in your own messaging app.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account/shop exists. To request deletion of your shop's data — including any staff profile you've filled in — contact us at contact.trustedgesolution@gmail.com.
